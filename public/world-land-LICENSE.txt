Local processing: WGS84 longitude/latitude coordinates rounded to two decimal places; adjacent duplicate points removed. The Antarctic polygon is split at longitude zero to avoid a 360-degree edge across the map. Small interior sea holes are retained in the separate holes array. The 110m source is already generalized; no additional geometric simplification was required.
Rendering: project each [longitude, latitude] point to your SVG coordinate system. Draw all polygons and holes as one compound path with fill-rule="evenodd", or subtract the holes. Every ring is closed. Do not use the coastline dataset as 1628 political borders.
